# ForeverCompanion

**The complete out-of-combat companion for WoW Forever.** English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Russian.

WoW Forever uses the Midnight addon rules: no addon can read combat. ForeverCompanion focuses on everything else, so you level faster, farm smarter and never lose track of your characters.

## Features

- **XP tracker**: XP per hour, time to level, rested XP, XP of quests ready to turn in.
- **Quest assistant**: the next quests to pick up, do and turn in for your race, class, faction and level (3,000 quests), nearest first, plus the dungeons for your level. Route recorder included.
- **Navigation arrow**: turns with your character and points to your next quest, a dungeon entrance or any waypoint, from one zone to another (`/fc way 45 62`).
- **Dungeon & raid guide**: every dungeon and raid, every boss with its level, health and loot with **drop rates**, every trash monster with its loot and rates, and the way to the entrance. Wishlist with an alert when your item drops.
- **Drop collector**: every corpse you loot is recorded. Share your data with `/fc drops export` to make the rates more accurate for everyone.
- **Alts & Legacy**: all your characters with gold, professions, lockouts and items. Find any item with `/fc find`. Counts your completed 1-60 journeys.
- **Camps**: camp buff timers and the camps you visited.
- **Bags**: sell junk, auto-repair, item level and on-use / on-equip markers.
- **Map**: icons on the world map for dungeon and raid entrances, quests to pick up and turn in, quest objectives, notes and camps. Coordinates on the world map and minimap.
- **Interface**: info bar (gold, bags, durability, latency, FPS), richer tooltips, clickable web links in chat.
- **Safety**: death log, durability and full-bag warnings, rare and dangerous elite alerts.
- **Leveling guide** (`/fc lvl`): a step-by-step route from 1 to 60 optimised for your race and class with the new WoW Forever quests and the dungeons to run at the right level, a reminder when a best-in-slot item of your level can be picked up, the 5 best items per slot every 5 levels for your class and faction (new Forever dungeons included) with where to get each, leveling talents, the spells you learn and when, and the rotation for your level.
- **Economy**: auction house prices (scan and prices seen while browsing), 14-day average, crafting cost and profit in tooltips, a gold ledger by source (loot, quests, vendor, auction house...), a shopping list and a 30-day gold chart (`/fc eco`).
- **PvP**: battleground results, honor and kills per session and per battleground, win rate (`/fc pvp`).
- **Hardcore**: warnings for zones and targets too dangerous for your level, enemy territory, a survival panel (deaths, potions, bandages, food, hearthstone, durability), a pre-pull checklist and your death history (`/fc hc`).

Every module can be turned off. Click the minimap button for the menu. Type `/fc` for the options and `/fc help` for all commands.

# ForeverCompanion (Français)

**Le compagnon complet hors combat pour WoW Forever.**

- **Suivi d'XP** : XP par heure, temps avant le niveau, repos, XP des quêtes prêtes à rendre.
- **Assistant de quêtes** : les prochaines quêtes à prendre, faire et rendre selon ta race, ta classe, ta faction et ton niveau, la plus proche d'abord, et les donjons de ton niveau.
- **Flèche de navigation** qui tourne avec ton personnage, vers ta prochaine quête, l'entrée d'un donjon ou un point de passage, d'une zone à l'autre.
- **Guide des donjons et raids** : tous les donjons et raids, chaque boss avec niveau, PV, butin et **taux de drop**, le butin du trash avec ses taux, et le chemin vers l'entrée. Liste de souhaits avec alerte.
- **Rerolls et Legacy** : or, métiers, verrouillages et objets de tous tes personnages.
- **Carte** avec les entrées de donjons, les quêtes et leurs objectifs. **Campements**, **sacs**, **interface** et **sécurité en monde ouvert**.
- **Guide de leveling** : route optimisée de 1 à 60 selon ta race et ta classe (nouvelles quêtes de Forever et donjons compris), rappel des objets BiS de ton niveau, meilleur équipement tous les 5 niveaux, talents, sorts et rotation de ta classe.
- **Économie** (prix de l'hôtel des ventes, coût et profit d'artisanat, registre d'or), **JcJ** (résultats des champs de bataille, honneur) et **Hardcore** (alertes de zones et cibles dangereuses, panneau de survie, historique des morts).

Tape `/fc` pour les options et `/fc help` pour toutes les commandes.
